Key Theoretical & Practical Principles of PCA
  • Why Standardize before PCA? PCA seeks directions that maximize variance. If Temperature (range 0–100°F) has higher numerical variance than Pressure (range 28–31 in), PCA will artificially dominate on Temperature. StandardScaler() gives all sensors equal weighting ($\mu=0, \sigma^2=1$).
  • Orthogonality: Every principal component is mathematically uncorrelated with every other component ($Cov(PC_i, PC_j) = 0$), completely eliminating multicollinearity.
  • Dimensionality Reduction: Retaining the top 2–3 components preserves >75% of the total dataset information while cutting the feature space in half.
